How Flips works

Each player is dealt 10 face-down cards. The starting player flips until their best five-card poker hand beats the other player's (with nothing showing, one card does it). Then the other player flips until they beat that, and play goes back and forth. Ties don't count as beating.

If you run out of cards without beating your opponent, they lead — but they still have to flip every remaining card, because the A♠ could be hiding in there.

2s are wild (optional): a 2 becomes whatever card gives you the best hand, including five of a kind. A♠ is an instant loss (optional): flip it and you lose. And since you must always play your best hand, if your best hand can only be made by turning a wild into the A♠ (say a spade royal flush or an ace-high spade flush), you lose too.

Hand ranking, high to low: Five of a Kind, Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Flush, Straight, Three of a Kind, Two Pair, Pair, High Card. Highlighted cards are the five that make your current hand.
